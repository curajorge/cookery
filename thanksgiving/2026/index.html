French red wine pot roast

Slow-braised beef with sweet onions, thyme and a gentle chile finish.

Serves 10  ·  Allow 4½–5½ hours  ·  Oven 300°F

Ingredients

  • Boneless beef chuckCut into 3 large pieces2,700 g
  • Fine saltDivide between beef and sauce25 g
  • Black pepperFreshly ground4 g
  • Neutral oilFor searing30 g
  • Yellow onionsThickly sliced700 g
  • CarrotsCut into large chunks500 g
  • GarlicChopped25 g
  • Tomato paste45 g
  • Crushed red pepperPlus 0.5 g for extra heat, if desired1 g
  • Dry red wineMerlot or Côtes du Rhône550 g
  • Unsalted beef stockUse enough to reach halfway up the beef750 g
  • Fresh thymeTie with kitchen twine5 g
  • Bay leavesAbout 2 leaves0.4 g

Method

  1. Season the beef

    Heat the oven to 300°F (150°C). Pat the chuck dry. Season all sides with 22 g salt and the black pepper. Reserve the remaining salt for the gravy.

  2. Brown in batches

    Heat the oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear one piece at a time for 3–5 minutes per broad side until deep brown. Transfer to a platter.

  3. Build the braise

    Lower the heat to medium. Soften the onions for 12–15 minutes. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste for 2 minutes. Add 1 g chile flakes for 20 seconds. Pour in the wine, scrape the browned bits loose, and simmer for 5 minutes.

  4. Cover and braise

    Nestle the beef into the onions. Add thyme, bay and enough stock to come halfway up the meat. Bring to a simmer, cover tightly and transfer to the oven. After 2 hours, tuck the carrots around the beef and cover again.

  5. Check for tenderness

    Start checking at 3 hours total oven time. A fork should turn easily in the thickest piece, typically around 195–205°F (91–96°C). If still firm, cover and check every 20 minutes. Rest the beef, loosely covered, for 20 minutes while finishing the gravy.

  6. Finish and serve

    Lift out the carrots and discard the herbs. Spoon excess fat from the sauce. Simmer for 10–20 minutes until it coats a spoon. Taste for salt and heat. For a spicier finish, add the extra 0.5 g flakes and simmer 5 minutes. Slice across the grain or pull into chunks and spoon the onion gravy over the top.
