# Puerto Rican–style BBQ chicken pinchos

Thanksgiving 2026 · Makes 20 short pinchos · Serves 10 alongside the roast

Prep 25 min · Marinate 1–12 hr · Griddle 10–14 min per batch

Garlicky adobo chicken thighs, a light barbecue glaze, and toasted bread on the tip of each skewer.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Boneless skinless chicken thighs | 900 g |
| Fresh garlic, mashed | 15 g |
| Neutral oil | 24 g |
| Apple cider vinegar | 20 g |
| Fine salt | 9 g |
| Dried oregano | 2 g |
| Sweet paprika | 3 g |
| Black pepper | 1 g |
| Barbecue sauce | 90 g |
| Baguette | 120 g |

Equipment: 20 short skewers and a flat-top griddle.

## Method

1. **Season the chicken.** Cut the chicken into roughly 25 mm pieces. Mash the garlic with the salt, then stir in oregano, paprika, pepper, 16 g oil and 15 g vinegar. Coat the chicken and refrigerate, covered, for 1–12 hours.

2. **Prepare the skewers.** Thread chicken loosely onto 20 short skewers, about 45 g per skewer. Leave a little space at the tip for bread. In a clean bowl, mix barbecue sauce with the remaining 5 g vinegar.

3. **Toast the bread.** Heat the flat top to about 375°F (190°C). Cut the baguette into 20 bite-size chunks, brush the cut faces with 4 g oil, and toast for 1–2 minutes. Set aside.

4. **Griddle and glaze.** Oil the flat top with the remaining 4 g oil. Lay the skewers flat in a single layer, working in batches. Turn every 2–3 minutes for even browning. Allow about 10–14 minutes per batch. Check the center of the thickest piece on each skewer for at least 165°F (74°C). Once cooked, brush lightly with the glaze and turn for 30–60 seconds to set it.

5. **Serve.** Rest 3 minutes, then slide a toasted bread chunk onto each tip. Serve warm, two pinchos per person alongside the roast.
