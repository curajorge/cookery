# French red wine pot roast

Thanksgiving 2026 · Serves 10 · Allow 4½–5½ hours

Slow-braised beef with sweet onions, thyme and a gentle chile finish.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Boneless beef chuck | 2,700 g | Cut into 3 large pieces |
| Fine salt | 25 g | Divide between beef and sauce |
| Black pepper | 4 g | Freshly ground |
| Neutral oil | 30 g | For searing |
| Yellow onions | 700 g | Thickly sliced |
| Carrots | 500 g | Cut into large chunks |
| Garlic | 25 g | Chopped |
| Tomato paste | 45 g |  |
| Crushed red pepper | 1 g | Plus 0.5 g for extra heat, if desired |
| Dry red wine | 550 g | Merlot or Côtes du Rhône |
| Unsalted beef stock | 750 g | Use enough to reach halfway up the beef |
| Fresh thyme | 5 g | Tie with kitchen twine |
| Bay leaves | 0.4 g | About 2 leaves |

## Method

1. **Season the beef.** Heat the oven to 300°F (150°C). Pat the chuck dry. Season all sides with 22 g salt and the black pepper. Reserve the remaining salt for the gravy.
2. **Brown in batches.** Heat the oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear one piece at a time for 3–5 minutes per broad side until deep brown. Transfer to a platter.
3. **Build the braise.** Lower the heat to medium. Soften the onions for 12–15 minutes. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste for 2 minutes. Add 1 g chile flakes for 20 seconds. Pour in the wine, scrape the browned bits loose, and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. **Cover and braise.** Nestle the beef into the onions. Add thyme, bay and enough stock to come halfway up the meat. Bring to a simmer, cover tightly and transfer to the oven. After 2 hours, tuck the carrots around the beef and cover again.
5. **Check for tenderness.** Start checking at 3 hours total oven time. A fork should turn easily in the thickest piece, typically around 195–205°F (91–96°C). If still firm, cover and check every 20 minutes. Rest the beef, loosely covered, for 20 minutes while finishing the gravy.
6. **Finish and serve.** Lift out the carrots and discard the herbs. Spoon excess fat from the sauce. Simmer for 10–20 minutes until it coats a spoon. Taste for salt and heat. For a spicier finish, add the extra 0.5 g flakes and simmer 5 minutes. Slice across the grain or pull into chunks and spoon the onion gravy over the top.
