# Rosemary Cold-Brew Limeade — Final Recipe

**Status:** Final fresh drink tasted and approved: “perfect.”  
**Use this recipe for future preparation.** It supersedes earlier provisional drink ratios in the planning notes. All ingredient amounts below are in grams.

## Serving amounts

| Ingredient | 1 small drink | 3 small drinks | 15 small drinks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cold-brew concentrate, undiluted | 40 | 120 | 600 |
| Fresh lime juice | 12 | 36 | 180 |
| Rosemary syrup | 18 | 54 | 270 |
| Shaking ice | 60 | 180 | 900 |

Provide additional fresh ice for the serving cups. These are small tasting portions, not full ten-ounce drinks.

## Make and serve

1. Keep the concentrate, syrup and freshly squeezed, strained lime juice cold in three separate containers.
2. Put the shaker on a scale and weigh in the shaking ice.
3. Tare to zero. Add coffee; tare. Add lime; tare. Add syrup.
4. Close and shake firmly for **15 seconds**.
5. Strain evenly into cups over fresh ice. Add a fresh rosemary sprig; a dried lemon wheel is an optional garnish.

For 15 drinks, make **five shaker batches of three**. Each batch holds 210 g liquids plus 180 g ice. Use a shaker with enough room for the ice to move; split into smaller batches if needed. Do not add dilution water to the concentrate or premix all service ingredients.

## Prepare the coffee concentrate

Coffee used: Panther Colombia Mejor, washed, medium-light roast. Grind medium-coarse.

| Brewing vessel | Ground coffee | Cold filtered water |
|---|---:|---:|
| Two-quart container | 200 | 1,000 |
| One-liter container | 100 | 500 |

Stir until all grounds are wet, cover and refrigerate for **18–20 hours**. Leave room to stir; do not tightly pack a filter basket. Strain out the grounds, then paper-filter through rinsed V60 filters. Combine the filtered batches, weigh, seal and refrigerate. Keep undiluted.

Recorded available concentrate: **781 g**. Allowing 80 g for testing leaves 701 g for service, enough for 17 drinks based on coffee alone. The chosen service target is 15; remaining syrup and lime stocks must also cover the table.

## Prepare the rosemary syrup

| Ingredient | Starting amount |
|---|---:|
| White cane sugar | 200 |
| Water | 200 |
| Washed fresh rosemary leaves and tender stems | 6 |
| Extra fresh rosemary, if needed | 2 |

1. Remove thick woody stems. Shake excess rinse water off before weighing the rosemary.
2. Heat sugar and water gently to about **80–85°C / 176–185°F**, stirring until dissolved. Avoid prolonged boiling.
3. Remove from heat. Add 6 g rosemary, cover and steep 10 minutes. Taste a cooled, lightly diluted spoonful; extend to 15 minutes if mild.
4. If still too mild, lightly bruise the additional 2 g rosemary and add it. Gently warm to **65–70°C / 149–158°F**, remove from heat and cover. Taste after 5 minutes; extend this second infusion up to 15 minutes if needed, stopping if woody or bitter.
5. Strain out all rosemary. Cool in an ice-water bath, keeping bath water out of the syrup. Transfer to a clean sealed container, weigh the finished syrup and refrigerate.

Development note: the syrup tasted mild alone but worked in the approved finished drink. The extra 2 g rosemary and exact final infusion duration were recommended during preparation but not independently confirmed as measured. Use aroma and taste to guide a repeat batch rather than treating an exact herb dose as verified.

## Supplies

Scale, shaker and strainer, three sealed ingredient containers, cups, ice, scoop/tongs, citrus squeezer, small strainer, knife and cutting board, rosemary garnish, optional dried lemon wheels. Keep ingredients refrigerated and transport chilled with ice packs.
