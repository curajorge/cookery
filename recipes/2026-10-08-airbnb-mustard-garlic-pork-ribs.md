## Mise en place

1. Preheat regular Bake oven to **375 F / 190 C**.
2. Unwrap ribs, pat dry. If a tough shiny membrane covers the bone side, loosen an edge and peel it away using a paper towel for grip.
3. Cut between bones into **3-4-rib sections** if necessary to fit the tray. Arrange in one layer; divide between trays if needed rather than stack.
4. Mix mustard, salt, pepper, finely minced garlic, paprika and optional sugar in one bowl. Do not taste raw-meat-contaminated coating.
5. Weigh 60 g water separately. Keep foil ready.

## Method

1. Coat both faces of the ribs with the mustard-seasoning mixture.
2. Arrange **bone side down, meaty side up** in the roasting tray. Pour **60 g water** into the bottom around the ribs.
3. Cover with **two complete layers of thin foil**, each tightly crimped all around. Fold any sheet-joining seams closed; no vent holes.
4. Bake in the preheated **375 F / 190 C** oven for **2 hours**. Start timing at actual oven entry. Thickness and oven performance can require longer.
5. Carefully uncover away from face. A skewer or thin knife between bones should enter with little resistance, and meat should have drawn back from bone ends. Probe thick meat avoiding bone: tenderness commonly occurs around **195-205 F / 90-96 C**. If resistant, reseal and bake **20-30 minutes more**, then recheck.
6. Once tender, remove foil and spoon a little tray juice over the ribs. Pour/ladle excess liquid into a separate heatproof container if necessary so ribs are not sitting in a deep pool. Bake **uncovered at the same 375 F** for **15-20 minutes**, checking at 10 minutes for darkening, until the coating gains color.
7. Rest **10 minutes**, loosely covered, then slice/serve. Expect small carryover, roughly **2-5 F**, variable; do not rely on carryover to soften tough meat.

## Quick Rationale

A tight foil seal retains moisture while connective tissue softens; the uncovered finish colors the surface. Mustard, garlic and paprika give a savory coating without requiring a barbecue sauce or a long marinade.
